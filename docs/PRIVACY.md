## What this tool collects — and doesn’t

**Your questionnaire answers stay in your browser.** The questionnaire runs entirely on your device and is saved only to your browser’s local storage so you can close the tab and continue later. Nothing is transmitted while you answer.

**Nothing is submitted anywhere unless you take one of two explicit actions:**

1. **AI personalization.** When you finish the questionnaire, we optionally send your answers (no name required — you never give one) to our server, which forwards them to Google’s Gemini API to write the personalized summary in your blueprint. The answers are used for that one request and are not stored on our servers. Do not enter customer-identifying information (names, addresses, job details of your customers) anywhere in the questionnaire — it asks only for approximate, business-level facts.
2. **A consultation request.** If you fill in the consultation form, we store exactly what you typed (name, email, company, optional message) plus your consent, the industry and size you selected, and your top findings — so the call can be useful. Stored only when the database is configured; otherwise your email program opens a pre-filled message you send yourself.

**What we never do:** sell your data, share it with third parties for marketing, run advertising trackers on this tool, or collect information about your customers.

## Data retention & deletion

- Questionnaire answers: in your browser only — clear them anytime with the “Start over” button or your browser’s site-data settings.
- Consultation requests: kept as long as the business relationship is relevant; email 【privacy@your-agency-domain.example】 to view or delete yours at any time.

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any consultation request you submitted. Contact 【privacy@your-agency-domain.example】 and we will respond within a reasonable period.

## Processors

- **Cloudflare** (hosting and serverless functions) — processes request data to operate the site.
- **Google (Gemini API)** — receives questionnaire answers for the optional personalization step, under Google’s data-processing terms for the Gemini API.
